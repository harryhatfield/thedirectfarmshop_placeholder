The idea

One marketplace, every farm in the country

TheDirectFarmShop is a single marketplace where farms across the UK list their own stock — picked, milked, baked, or collected today — priced plainly and updated as it sells. Browse it like your usual online supermarket shop: everything's sorted into categories, so you pick items and quantities and check out — except every item comes from a named farm instead of a warehouse, and the whole country's on there, sorted by what's closest to you first.

One marketplace

Every farm sells through the same listings — no separate sites or accounts, just one place to shop.

Sorted by distance

The whole country's on there, closest farms first. Widen it, or narrow it to just your patch.

Collection or delivery

Some farms deliver UK-wide, some are collection-only — marked clearly on every listing.
